<h1 align="center">Regulations of Champions Cup 2026</h1>

## 1. Introduction

1. *Champions Cup 2026* (hereinafter referred to as Tournament) is tournament played in the game Heroes of Might and 
   Magic III with the Horn of the Abyss expansion (hereinafter referred to as Heroes III). The purpose of the 
   Tournament is to organize a spectacle in the form of matches between the best players of H3gg tournaments and 
   Liga Heroes in 2025. Champions Cup 2026 is a continuation of Champions Cup 2024 and Champions Cup 2023, which 
   took place in 2024 and 2025. Official original name of the Tournament is "Puchar Zdobywców Pucharów 2026".

2. The Tournament is organized without any profit for the Organizers. All rights to the Heroes III brand, logo and trademarks 
   belong to Ubisoft Entertainment SA.

3. The rules governing the Tournament (hereinafter referred to as Tournament Rules) are constituted by this document 
   (hereinafter referred to as Regulations), Documents listed in [point 4. of chapter 1.](#point-1-4) and Official 
   Announcements of the organizers specified in [point 8. of chapter 1.](#point-1-8).

4. <a id="point-1-4"></a>Documents being part of the Tournament Rules:

    - [Privacy Policy](./Documents/Privacy_Policy.md)
    - [Templates Regulation](./Documents/Templates_Regulations.md)


5. Following hierarchy of the Tournament Rules applies:\
   `1)` Regulations,\
   `2)` Documents listed in [point 4. chapter 1.](#point-1-4),\
   `3)` Official Announcements.

    - **Participants** or **Participant**, it means, depending on the context: persons taking part in any phase of the Tournament, persons taking part in a given Match or, in the case of singular number: each individual person taking part in any phase of the Tournament or person taking part in the Match,

8. <a id="point-1-8"></a> The Organizers inform about decisions made within the Tournament in the form of Official Announcements. Official Announcements will be published on the Discord Server on the `#organizers-announcements` channel.

9. Each Participant has the right to appeal any decision made by the Organizers, no later than 14 days from the publication of the Official Announcement in which the decision was made. The method of appeal is defined as follows:

   `1)` A Participant who wants to appeal a decision of the Organizers sends to one of the Organizers, in case it was an individual decision of one of the Organizers, to another Organizer than the one who made that decision, information about the appeal containing the following information:
   <ul style="list-style: none;">

   `2)` The Organizers inform the Participant about the consideration of the appeal within 7 days of receiving the appeal,

   `3)` In case the appeal is considered positively, the Organizers will promptly do everything in their power to reverse the effects of the appealed decision, in case the decision is crucial for continuing the Tournament, they will also make a substitute decision, taking into account the suggestion of the appealing Participant.

10. The decision of the Organizers is binding in situations not covered by these regulations. Those decision will be published in Official Announcements.

11. Organizers reserve the right to not allow a selected person to perform shoutcaster or stream host function in transmission from Game played in Tournament.

12. The Organizers reserve the right to change the Tournament Rules in cases where it is necessary for the proper course of the Tournament. The Organizers are obliged to inform about changes to the Tournament Rules in an official announcement. Changes come into effect on the of the publication, except for changes to the Privacy Policy, which come into effect after 14 days from the publication of the decision.

13. Polish version of the Tournament Rules is the official version. In case of discrepancies between the Polish version and another language version, the Polish version shall prevail. The official original name of the Tournament is "Puchar Zdobywców Pucharów 2026".

## 2. Tournament Format

1. The Tournament will be divided into 2 phases:

   `1)` Group Phase,

   `2)` Grand Finals.

2. Finalists of the following H3gg and Liga Heroes tournaments will be invited to participate in the Tournament:
    - XL+yoU 3,
    - Mirror Casino 4,
    - 30th Anniversary Cup,
    - King of h3dm,
    - Champions Cup 2024,
    - Liga Heroes Season IX and X - winners of divisions 1a and 1b,
    - Liga Mistrzów Liga Heroes Season IX,
    - Taste of Haste 3 - TURBO is back,
    - Exotic Tournament II,
    - Sapphire Battlegrounds 2,
    - "Six it up!" - Knockout Stage - Division PRO.

3. Group Phase:
   `1)` Group Phase will last from January 14, 2026 to February 11, 2026 inclusive,

   `2)` All Participants invited to the Tournament based on point 2. of this chapter will take part in the Group Phase,

   `3)` In the Group Phase, Participants will be divided into 2 groups of equal size. In each group, Participants will play Matches against each other in a round-robin system.

   `4)` For a win, a Participant receives 1 point, in case of a loss it is 0 points, in case of a draw the Match should be repeated,

   `5)` The winners of the Group Phase will be 2 Participants with the highest number of points in each group,

   `6)` In case two or more Participants in one group score an equal number of points, the result of direct Matches between these Participants decides,

   `7)` In case where the result of direct Matches does not allow to unambiguously indicate the winners (e.g. three Participants A, B and C are in one group and have an equal number of points, Participant A won the direct Match against Participant B, B against C, and C against A) the Organizers will decide the order in the group by coinflip according to paragraph `8)`,

   `8)` The coinflip is conducted by one of the Organizers in the presence of all Participants to whom it applies. The entire procedure should be recorded by the Organizer.

   `9)` Paragraphs `6)` and `7)` apply only in cases where they are necessary to determine the winners of the Group Phase.

4. Grand Finals:
   `1)` Grand Finals will last from February 12, 2026 to March 12, 2026 inclusive,

   `2)` 4 winners of the Group Phase will take part in the Grand Finals,

   `3)` Grand Finals will be held in the form of a Bo3 bracket between the winners of the Group Phase,

   `4)` In the first phase of the Grand Finals, Participants will face each other in pairs, where the winner of group 1 will face the player in 2nd place in group 2, and the winner of group 2 will face the player in 2nd place in group 1,

   `5)` The winners of the first phase of the Grand Finals will face each other in the Grand Final,

   `6)` The deadline for playing each Match in the Grand Finals is 14 days.

5. In case of issues with playing a Match on time:

   `1)` at the request of a Participant of the Match, the Organizers may extend the deadline for playing the Match by one day,

   `2)` at the request of both Participants of the Match and with the consent of the Participant with whom the winner of the Match would face, in which there is an issue with playing on time, the Organizers may extend the deadline for playing the Match by more than one day, provided that Participants should previously agree on the date of the next round Match with their potential opponent in it, and then provide it to the Organizers,

6. In case of issues with agreeing on a date of a Match, the Organizers, at the request of one of the Participants of the Match or on their own decision, may set a referee date for a given Match.

7. n case of many issues with playing a Match on time or other circumstances that, in the Organizers' opinion, require such a decision, the deadline for each phase may be extended.

## 4. Gameplay


1. Games should be played through HotA Lobby available in Heroes III. If HotA Lobby is unavailable, the Game can be played through Hamachi or Game Ranger.

2. Preparation of the Game:

   `1)` One of the Participants creates a room in HotA Lobby (they will be the host), the other Participant joins the room created by the host in the Lobby (they will be the guest).

   `2)` The guest chooses digit 1 or 0, the host uses the `Coin Flip` option to randomly select digit 1 or 0.

3. Templates eligible for the Tournament and the method of their selection are specified in the [Templates Regulation](./Documents/Templates_Regulations.md).

4. Color and town selection: 

   `1)` On non-mirror templates (6lm10a, 8xm12a, Apocalypse, Conquest, Diamond, Jebus Cross, Nostalgia, Sapphire XH, Spider):
   <ul style="list-style: none;">

   a) Towns are randomly selected via Random Town vs Random Town option, in case of drawing two identical towns, drawing should be repeated, 

   b) Participants can request a reroll of towns and ban 0-2 selected towns for a fee of 500 gold. The first Participant to request a reroll is the winner of the coin flip. Each Participant is entitled to one such ban per Game. If the same pair of towns is selected as before the reroll, the selection should be repeated,

   c) The bidding for town and color starts with the Participant who won the coin flip. The first bidding concerns towns, the second concerns colors.

   d) The winner of the town bidding chooses one of the randomly selected towns,

   e) The winner of the color bidding chooses one of the colors: red or blue,

   f) The starting hero is chosen first by the red player.
   </ul>

   `2)` On mirror templates (h3dm1, mt_BrokenMirror, mt_Nebula)
   <ul style="list-style: none;">

   a) Town is drawn using Random Town option,

   b) Participants can request a reroll of town and banning 0-1 drawn towns by 500 gold fee. The winner of the coin flip is the first to declare their willingness to reroll. There is a maximum of one such ban per Participtant per Match,

   c) The bidding for color begins with the Participant, who won the coin flip.

   d) The bidding for color winner chooses one of the colors: red or blue,

   f) Participants choose starting heroes on mirror templates according to point `3)` below.
   </ul>

   `3)` Selecting starting heroes on mirror templates (where player A is winner of bidding for color):
   <ul style="list-style: none;">
   a) player A bans 0-3 heroes,

   b) player B chooses one of the remaining starting heroes in the drawn faction,

   c) player A chooses one of the remaining starting heroes in the drawn faction.
   </ul>

## 5. Restarty

1. On all mirror templates, each Participant has at their disposal in a given Game one subjective restart on the first day (111). On other templates, each Participant has at their disposal in a given Game two subjective restarts on the first day (2 x 111) or one restart on the second day (1 x 112).

2. First to use a subjective restart is the red player, then the blue player. The blue player has the right to use a subjective restart in the second and third day, counted as a subjective restart from the previous day, up to 5 seconds after accepting the prompt to start their turn.

3. After subjective restarts are used, with the consent of both Participants, or in a situation where they cannot reach an agreement, with the Organizers' consent, they can also use technical restarts. Technical restarts can be used if:

   `1)` Some towns has not been generated,

   `2)` The starting hero is different from the one selected in the lobby,

   `3)` Some roads have not been generated,

   `4)` Template settings are different from those listed in point 2. of chapter 3 of the Templates Regulations, except for the settings listed in point 1. of chapter 2 of the Templates Regulations, in which case these settings should be changed before restarting the map,

   `5)` All available expansion roads through road connections are blocked by guards that cannot be bypassed, or bypassing them requires using at least 2000 movement points on each guard, and defeating them causes losses of at least 70% of the starting army (defined as the total HP of the army available at the start and possible to purchase in the first turn of the game) with optimal battle play and assuming neutral morale, for each guard. This does not apply to zone guards,

   `6)` All available expansion roads through road connections are blocked by zone guards with a value of at least 8000, which cannot be bypassed, or bypassing them requires using at least 2000 movement points on each guard, and defeating them causes losses of at least 70% of the army (defined as the total HP of the army available at the start and possible to purchase from dwellings located within a maximum distance of 2000 movement points from the road and possible to occupy by the day of reporting the restart, as well as cities including armies available from dwellings in cities that can be purchased using resources located within a maximum distance of 1500 movement points from the road and possible to collect by the day of reporting the restart) with optimal battle play and assuming neutral morale, for each guard,

   `7)` All available expansion roads through road connections are blocked, some by guards from point `5)` and some by guards from point `6)`.

    - technical restarts can be used up to fifth day of the Game (115),

    > [!IMPORTANT]
    > Organizers recommends that after using subjective restarts, one of the judges should be sent a Game save and passwords to it from both Participants, in order to check the availability of technical restarts on a given generation.

4. After subjective restarts are used, Participants can use early meeting restarts. They are available if there is an interaction between Participants in turn 115 for fast and 116 for medium roads, each Participant has the right to request an early meeting restart, but it is not mandatory. Interaction between Participants is defined as any fight between Participants or taking over an object belonging to the opponent. Early meeting restarts can only be used at the first interaction between Participants in a given generation.

5. None of the restarts can be used to change the selected starting town, starting hero or starting bonus.

## 6. Ending the Match and the Game

1. After the Game is completed, the losing Participant must write "gg" in the Game chat (and in case of playing in a ranked mode, confirm the admission of defeat). This is equivalent to admitting defeat and serves as proof confirming the Game report. The decision to surrender the Game cannot be reverted.

2. Organizers at the request of both Participants, or on its own decision, may end the Game by arbitrarily indicating the winner based on the assessment of the situation in the Game.

3. After the Game is completed, Participants are required to give passwords that protect the Game saves.

4. The winner submits a Match report on the Tournament Website, if the losing Participant expresses such a wish, they can submit a report on behalf of the winner.

5. If report was not confirmed within the deadline of the Match and no fraud was reported in the report, it is considered confirmed.

6. Match ends when all reports from its Games are confirmed.

## 7. Punishments

1. The Organizers have several options at their disposal to punish Participants for non-compliance with the Regulations. The penalty levels are defined as follows:

   `1)` Warning - given for small infractions. First warning does not lead to any other consequences, but each next does:
   <ul style="list-style: none">
   a) Second warning - walkover for Game (Match in case of Bo1)

   b) Third warning - disqualification
   </ul>
   `2)` Walkover for Game (Match in case of Bo1) - given for medium infractions, including not meeting the deadlines,

   `3)` Disqualification - given for major infractions,

2. The Organizers reserve the right to disqualify a Participant due to a serious offense committed in another Heroes III tournament.

3. Organizers in cases where irregularities related to playing a Game occurred, can order repeating it, or, in case where Game is still going, stoping it.

4. If one of the Participants fails to show up on the scheduled date of the Game, the following procedure should be used (whenever the procedure includes phrases in the second person, the Organizers mean the Participant playing in a given Game who is waiting for his late opponent):

   `1)` After 5 minutes from the scheduled time, inform the Organizers that you are starting this procedure, then message your opponent on private message with EXACTLY the following text, without quotes: “I have attempted to play a Game with you, and now I am waiting for you to show up. According to the rules, you have 15 minutes from now to show up and start playing the game, or otherwise you will be penalized with a game forfeit. I have informed the Organizers that this procedure has begun."

   `2)` <a id="point-7-3-2"></a>After 15 minutes from the scheduled time, if the opponent has not shown up yet, you have to inform the Organizers of this situation, then message your opponent with EXACTLY the following text, without quotes: “You have not shown up as requested. I have informed the Organizer of this situation. I am waiting another 15 minutes for your response, but according to regulations you will be devoid of subjective restarts in this Game.”

   `3)` If the opponent does show up after sending the message in [paragraph `2)`](#point-7-3-2) and before the 30 minutes after scheduled time, they won't be able to use subjective restarts in this Game.

   `4)` After 30 minutes from the scheduled time, if the opponent has not shown up yet, you have to inform the Organizers of this situation, then message your opponent with EXACTLY the following text, without quotes: “You have not shown up as requested. I have informed the Organizer of this situation. I am not waiting for your respone any longer.”

   `5)` If the opponent does show up and is willing to start the Game immediately, you should allow for the necessary setup time. If the opponent is visibly stalling for time, you should report this to the Organizers. As a rule of thumb, you should allow for at least 5 extra minutes to let the opponent connect to the online lobby and join the room.

   `6)` If this was a Game scheduled by the Organizers, and you neglect to inform the Staff about the fact that your opponent is missing, a double game forfeit may be given at Organizers discretion.

   `7)` You may delay this procedure up to an hour if you wish to give more time to your opponent - but you are not required to do so.

   `8)` For the procedure listed above, you have to provide proof in the form of a screenshot that you have messaged your opponent and they have not responded on time. Additionally, you should provide the proof of the scheduled date and time for the Game. If this procedure was used for an ambiguously scheduled Game, the Organizers may decide to not penalize the missing player depending on the full context. The Organizers may also decide to reduce the severity of the penalty in case of force majeure.
