## 2. Template selection

1. Selecting template from template pack:
   `1)` In Bo1 Match, Participants eliminate template pack in BA system, where A is winner of the coinflip, until 
   one template pack remains. Then Participants eliminate templates in ABBA system, until one template on which the 
   Game will be played remains, 

   `2)` In case of Bo3 Match:
   <ul style="list-style: none;">

   a) coinflip winner selects template pack, then Participants eliminate template in ABBA system, until one template 
   on which the first Game will be played remains 

   b) coinflip loser selects template pack, then Participants eliminate template in ABBA system, until one template
   on which the second Game will be played remains

   c) Participants eliminate templates from remaining pack in ABBA system, until one template
   on which the third Game will be played remains
   </ul>

   `3)` In Bo3 Match, each Game should be played with selection of different template pack.

2. Template selection in case when as a result of the procedure from point 1. of this chapter, Random template is selected:

   `1)` Participants by using `Random Town` option draw one town,

   `2)` drawn town corresponds to given template from Random template pool:

3. The selection of computer players' towns through bidding, should be carried out after players' town bidding and proceeds as follows:

    `1)` The host randomly selects 6 unique towns, different from those chosen by the Participants. The towns chosen as 1st, 2nd, and 3rd form the "first AI pack," and those chosen as 4th, 5th, and 6th form the "second AI pack".\
    `2)` Participants bid for the priority of choosing the AI pack.\
    `3)` The winner of the bid for the AI pack first chooses which AI factions will be assigned to "their" computer players - those from the first or second pack. The assignment of computer players is based on color:
    - The red player "owns":
      - Brown
      - Orange
      - Light Blue
    - The b~lue player "owns":
      - Green
      - Purple
      - Pink

   `4)` Starting from the blue player, players place towns from their auctioned pack for their computer players.\
   `5)` The selection of towns for computer players takes place after all other auctions are completed.
